Our Story

Tradition Served
with a Modern Soul

❖

Named after the Xinhai Revolution of 1911 — a turning point that transformed China — our restaurant honours that spirit of bold change. We bring the soul of Chinese culinary tradition to the heart of Tallinn, at the crossroads of old Kalamaja.

Every dish tells a story: hand-pulled Biang Biang noodles from Shaanxi, slow-braised crispy pork belly, wonton soups made from recipes passed through generations. Our kitchen bridges centuries of technique with contemporary Nordic sensibility.

This is not fast food. This is slow pleasure — the art of sitting, tasting, and letting time pause for a moment.

The Full Experience

"Food is not just sustenance — it is ceremony, memory, and the most honest form of cultural expression."

At Xinhai1911, we believe the dining table is sacred. Every element — the lighting, the sound of broth, the clatter of chopsticks, the warmth of a hand-thrown bowl — is considered. We are not just a restaurant. We are a place to slow down, to connect, to taste something true.

Located in the vibrant Kalamaja neighbourhood of Tallinn, our space bridges the creative energy of the district with the timeless ritual of Chinese dining.
